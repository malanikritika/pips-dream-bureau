Lantern Night

The lanterns are asleep in the dark. Move your mouse or finger to carry a small light through the night. When you spot a lantern, click or tap it to wake it up.

Light every lantern before the timer runs out. Stuck? Wait a few seconds and a faint ripple will point the way.
