Chapter 01: The First Dream

On a quiet night, beneath a sky full of stars, Pip discovered a tiny glowing moonbeam tucked inside a cloud. It hummed the softest melody Pip had ever heard. That little melody sparked a big idea: what if every creature could have a beautiful dream of its own?

With a music box, a handful of moon dust, and an enormous imagination, Pip began creating lullabies and glowing nightlights. Soon, the whole neighborhood was drifting into peaceful dreams. And so began Pip's adventures as a Junior Dream Engineer.
